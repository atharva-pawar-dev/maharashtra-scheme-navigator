## 2. Demonstration Governance & Truthfulness Invariants

1. **Synthetic Data Only:** The demonstration strictly utilizes verified synthetic citizen profiles (e.g. *Sunita Patil, Ramesh Jadhav, Vijay Shinde*). **Zero real citizen personal data, Aadhaar numbers, or bank records are ever used.**
2. **Clear Demonstration Watermark:** All demonstration assets carry the subtle notice:  
   *"Scheme Navigator — Independent Technology Demonstration — Synthetic Data"*.
3. **No Implied Endorsement:** The presentation makes zero claims of official government partnership, CSC accreditation, or political endorsement. It presents an independent technology proposal designed to assist citizens and evaluate potential future interoperability.
4. **Deterministic Reproducibility:** Every scenario is test-backed and reproducible locally on `http://127.0.0.1:8000`.
