## 1. Executive Overview

This document defines the comprehensive scheme universe for the **Maharashtra Scheme Navigator** platform. Rather than restricting the catalogue to a single portal or a handful of high-profile schemes, Scheme Navigator establishes an evidence-backed taxonomy encompassing all officially documented citizen-benefit programmes available to residents of Maharashtra.

The platform rigorously separates welfare entitlements from public services and application channels:

## 2. Seven Structural Tiers of the Scheme Universe

### 2.1 Tier A: Maharashtra State Government Schemes
Schemes conceptualized, legislated, and funded 100% by the State Government of Maharashtra under operative Government Resolutions (शासन निर्णय):
- **Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana:** ₹1,500/month direct honorarium for eligible women aged 21–65 from families with annual income $\le$ ₹2.5 Lakh (Women & Child Development Dept, GR 28-06-2024 & amendments).
- **Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Anudan Yojana (SGNAY):** Monthly assistance for destitute persons, orphans, divorced women, and persons with certified disability $\ge 40\%$ (Social Justice Dept, GR 20-08-2019, GR 05-07-2023, GR 15-09-2025).
- **Shravanbal Seva Rajya Nivruttivetan Yojana (Group B):** 100% state-funded pension of ₹1,500/month for destitute senior citizens aged 65+ with income $\le$ ₹21,000/year (Social Justice Dept).
- **Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti Yojna (EBC Waiver):** 50% tuition and exam fee reimbursement for Economically Backward Class students with family income $\le$ ₹8 Lakh (Higher & Technical Education Dept).
- **100% Tuition Fee Waiver for Girls in Higher & Technical Education:** Free education for girls from OBC, EWS, and SEBC families with income $\le$ ₹8 Lakh enrolled in approved professional degree/diploma courses (Higher & Technical Education Dept, GR 08-07-2024).

### 2.2 Tier B: Central Government Schemes Available in Maharashtra
Central Sector (CS) schemes funded 100% by the Government of India, available to residents of Maharashtra through federal portals or direct bank transfer:
- **PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi):** ₹6,000/year in three four-monthly installments of ₹2,000 for landholding farmer families.
- **PM Mudra Yojana:** Micro-enterprise loans up to ₹10–20 Lakh under Shishu, Kishore, and Tarun categories.
- **National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS):** Stipend support for youth undergoing industry apprenticeship.
- **Central Sector Scholarship for College and University Students:** Merit scholarships administered via National Scholarship Portal (NSP).

### 2.3 Tier C: Joint Central-State Schemes (Centrally Sponsored Schemes - CSS)
Schemes governed by joint funding ratios between the Union Government and the Government of Maharashtra:
- **Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS) / Shravanbal Group A:** Central component (₹200 for ages 65–79, ₹500 for ages 80+) topped up by Maharashtra State Government (₹1,300 / ₹1,000) to ensure a total pension of ₹1,500/month.
- **Government of India Post-Matric Scholarship for SC Students:** 60:40 funding ratio between Union Ministry of Social Justice and Government of Maharashtra, providing compulsory fee reimbursement and maintenance allowances.
- **Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY-Gramin & PMAY-Urban):** 60:40 funding ratio for rural and urban pucca housing assistance.
- **Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY & Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (MJPJAY):** Joint integrated cashless healthcare scheme providing ₹5 Lakh annual family coverage across empanelled hospitals in Maharashtra.
- **Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan:** 60:40 funding ratio covering elementary, secondary, and teacher education infrastructure.

### 2.5 Tier E: State Development Corporations, Boards & Autonomous Institutes
Autonomous corporations set up under specific statutory mandates providing loans, interest refunds, and skill fellowships:
- **Mahatma Phule Backward Class Development Corporation (MPBCDC):** Direct micro loans and bank-subsidized 50% capital assistance for Scheduled Caste entrepreneurs.
- **Annasaheb Patil Arthik-Magas Vikas Mahamandal (AMVDC):** Interest reimbursement scheme (IR2) up to ₹10–15 Lakh loan for youth entrepreneurs from Maratha / Economically Weak Sections.
- **Vasantrao Naik Vimukta Jati & Nomadic Tribes Development Corporation:** Seed money and margin capital assistance for VJNT artisans and small trades.
- **Shabari Adivasi Vitta Va Vikas Mahamandal:** Commercial vehicle loans and business finance for Scheduled Tribe individuals.
- **Maulana Azad Minorities Financial Development Corporation (MAMFDC):** Education loans up to ₹20 Lakh (overseas) and term business loans at 3% concessional interest for notified minorities.
- **MahaJyoti (Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Research & Training Institute):** Free UPSC/MPSC coaching, PhD research fellowships, and laptop distributions for OBC/VJNT/SBC scholars.
- **BARTI (Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Research & Training Institute):** Higher education fellowships and administrative service exam training for SC candidates.
- **TRTI (Tribal Research & Training Institute):** Tribal youth skill training, competitive exam guidance, and ethnographic documentation.
- **SARTHI (Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Research, Training & Human Development Institute):** Fellowships and skill programs for Maratha/Kunbi students.
- **Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board (BOCWW):** ₹5,000 tool kit allowance, ₹10,000–₹50,000 academic scholarships, and maternity benefits for registered construction workers with 90-day active labor certificates.

### 2.6 Tier F: District and Municipal Local Bodies
- **36 District Collectorates:** Taluka-level Sanjay Gandhi Yojana Committees sanctioning monthly destitute pensions.
- **Zilla Parishads (ZPs):** District-level agricultural implement distribution, animal husbandry subsidy, rural sanitation grants.
- **Municipal Corporations (e.g., BMC, PMC, PCMC, NMMC, Nagpur, Nashik, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar):** Municipal education scholarships, street vendor self-employment support under PM SVANidhi, and urban health clinics.

## 3. Clear Demarcation: Schemes vs. Public Services vs. Channels

| Aspect | Scheme Registry | Public Service Registry | Application Channel Registry |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary Definition** | Welfare benefit, subsidy, pension, scholarship, or financial entitlement. | Notified administrative certificate or legal record under Maharashtra RTS Act. | Official portal, counter, or authority where an application is legally filed. |
| **Examples** | Majhi Ladki Bahin, Post-Matric SC Scholarship, SGNAY, KUSUM Solar Pump. | Domicile Certificate, Tahsildar Income Certificate, Caste Validity, 7/12 Extract. | MahaDBT 2.0, Aaple Sarkar RTS, Taluka Tehsildar Counter, Anganwadi. |
| **Output to Citizen** | Direct cash transfer, tuition waiver, pension, or asset subsidy. | Digitally signed legal certificate proving an attribute (e.g. income, age, residence). | Submission acknowledgement slip, application ID, or physical receipt. |
| **Legal Role** | Target objective sought by the citizen. | Prerequisite evidence required to satisfy statutory conditions. | Operational route to submit the application. |

Connecting these three registries prevents citizen confusion: a citizen is never misled into believing that obtaining an Income Certificate is a welfare scheme itself; instead, the system explains that the Income Certificate is an Aaple Sarkar public service required as a prerequisite to apply for the Majhi Ladki Bahin or Post-Matric Scholarship scheme.

## 4. Reality Audit: Discovered Raw Records vs. Canonical Unique Schemes

### 4.1 What the 1,319 Discovered Records Genuinely Represent
The figure of **1,319 discovered records** frequently cited in initial discovery reports represents the **aggregate total of raw crawled items, Government Resolutions, notified public services, portal search indices, and directory entries** across all 72 monitored official sources. 

These 1,319 items DO NOT represent 1,319 distinct welfare schemes. They are raw source artifacts:

| Source Artifact Type | Primary Official Source | Raw Count | Description & Nature of Artifacts |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Government Resolutions (GRs)** | `gr.maharashtra.gov.in` | 420 | Scanned / digital executive orders amending, framing, or funding schemes. |
| **Notified Public Services (RTS)** | `aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in` | 504 | Administrative certificates and services under Maharashtra RTS Act 2015. |
| **Unified Portal Services** | `mahasarathi.maharashtra.gov.in` | 150 | Departmental services indexed for upcoming MahaID SSO integration. |
| **Scholarship & Benefit Entries** | `mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in` (1.0 & 2.0) | 122 | Course-specific and department-specific scholarship intake rows. |
| **National Search Directory Leads** | `myscheme.gov.in` | 112 | High-level national aggregator index entries for Maharashtra. |
| **State Corporation Programs** | MPBCDC, AMVDC, BARTI, MahaJyoti | 87 | Loan, micro-credit, stipend, and coaching listings. |
| **District & Municipal Welfare Items** | 6 Collectorates & 6 Municipalities | 65 | Local welfare line items (differently-abled kits, sanitation grants). |
| **Central / Centrally Sponsored Schemes** | Central Ministries / Portals | 15 | Central sector and CSS listings applicable in Maharashtra. |
| **State Scheme Budget Lines** | MahaScheme / Planning Department | 95 | Departmental line-item budget entries across fiscal years. |
| **Total Raw Source Records** | **72 Monitored Sources** | **1,569 (1,319 core)** | **Gross crawled / indexed document artifacts.** |
