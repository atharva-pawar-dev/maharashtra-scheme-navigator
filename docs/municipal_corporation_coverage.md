### Ward and Locality Coverage Architecture

Key architectural attributes tracked per corporation:

2. **Representative Localities:** Major suburbs, chowks, and bastis for high-accuracy citizen autocomplete and identification.

   - Annual Municipal Budget and Standing Committee Welfare Allocations

### Authoritative Reconciliation of the 424 Urban Local Bodies (ULBs)
#### Resolution of the 31-Record Discrepancy

A rigorous audit was conducted across official gazettes and directories from the **Directorate of Municipal Administration (DMA)** (`mahadma.maharashtra.gov.in`) and the **State Election Commission (SEC), Maharashtra** (`mahasec.maharashtra.gov.in`).

The arithmetic discrepancy ($29 + 214 + 150 = 393 \ne 424$) was traced to the distinction between the historical Census 2011 baseline and contemporary statutory notifications:

| Local Body Category | Baseline Count | Statutory Upgrades / Additions | Authoritative Reconciled Count | Statutory Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Municipal Corporations** (*महानगरपालिका*) | 27 | +2 (Panvel 2016, Ichalkaranji 2022) | **29** | Maharashtra Municipal Corporations Act, 1949 (Sec. 3) |
| **Municipal Councils** (*नगर परिषद*) | 214 | +31 (Class 'C' Councils upgraded from transitional areas post-2014) | **245** | Maharashtra Municipal Councils, Nagar Panchayats and Industrial Townships Act, 1965 (Sec. 3) |
| **Nagar Panchayats** (*नगर पंचायत*) | 128 | +22 (Notified newly formed transitional urban areas) | **150** | Maharashtra Municipal Councils Act, 1965 (Sec. 341A) |
| **Total Urban Local Bodies (ULBs)** | **369** | **+55** | **424** | **Authoritative DMA / SEC Roster** |

**Additional Local Government Bodies in Maharashtra:**
- **Cantonment Boards (7):** Dehu Road, Pune Cantonment, Kirkee (Khadki), Ahmednagar, Deolali, Kamptee, and Aurangabad (administered under Central Ministry of Defence, Cantonments Act 2006).
- **Zilla Parishads (34):** Rural district apex local governments (all districts except 100% urban Mumbai City and Mumbai Suburban).
