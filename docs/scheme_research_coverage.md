### 1. Research Scope and Verification Methodology

Every scheme entry within the Scheme Navigator platform is subjected to a strict 4-point verification protocol before publication:
1. **Primary Statutory Evidence:** Verification against published Maharashtra Government Resolutions (GRs) hosted on `maharashtra.gov.in`, state gazettes, or central ministry notifications.
2. **Channel & Portal Validation:** Live inspection of the designated public delivery portal (e.g., Aaple Sarkar, MahaDBT, PM-Kisan portal, municipal ERP portals).
3. **Statutory RTS Alignment:** Cross-referencing service delivery time limits against notifications under the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015 (महाराष्ट्र लोकसेवा हक्क अधिनियम २०१५).
4. **Data Integrity & Non-Fabrication:** Strict exclusion of unofficial aggregator hearsay, social media rumors, or approximate dates. Where statutory rules differ across GR amendments, the latest active amendment is cited.
