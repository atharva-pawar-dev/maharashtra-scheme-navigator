## 1. Statutory Advisory Disclaimers & Non-Government Affiliation

To preserve public trust, prevent citizen confusion, and comply with state and central legal standards, Maharashtra Scheme Navigator explicitly and prominently disclaims executive authority across all digital touchpoints:

### 1.1 Non-Government Sanctioning Authority Notice
- **Statutory Status:** Scheme Navigator is an **independent, informational and advisory platform**.
- **No Sanctioning Authority:** The platform does NOT issue government entitlements, grant official sanctions, approve benefits, or disburse public funds.
- **Sole Jurisdiction:** Final eligibility determination, document scrutiny, and benefit disbursement rest **exclusively** with the competent authorities of the Government of Maharashtra and the Government of India.
- **Source Documents:** Government Resolutions (GRs) and scheme circulars linked on the platform are provided for public transparency and provenance; retrieval or indexing by MSN does NOT constitute official endorsement or guarantee of benefits.
