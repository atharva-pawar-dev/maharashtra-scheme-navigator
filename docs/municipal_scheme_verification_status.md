# Municipal Scheme Verification & Legal Audit Status
## Statutory Citations, Legal Resolutions & Publication Clearance

### 1. Verification Audit Framework
Every municipal and local government scheme ingested into Scheme Navigator undergoes a three-stage legal and administrative verification:
1. **Resolution & Legal Authority Audit:** Verification of Municipal Corporation Standing Committee Resolution (`स्थायी समिती ठराव`) or General Body Resolution (`सर्वसाधारण सभा ठराव`).
2. **Citizen Application Channel Integrity:** Verification that the official online or offline application route is active, valid, and accepts citizens.
3. **Publication Status Clearance:** Assignment of publication clearance (`VERIFIED_FOR_INFORMATION`, `VERIFIED_FOR_ELIGIBILITY`, or `PUBLISHED`).

### 3. Verification Status Taxonomy Across Maharashtra Local Bodies

The Scheme Navigator directory enforces a four-tier verification classification across all 29 Municipal Corporations:

1. **`VERIFIED` (5 Corporations / 13 Municipal Schemes + 1 Central + 1 Council + 1 ZP):**
   - Active legal authority cited (Standing Committee / General Body / GR reference).
   - Direct citizen application channel verified (Online portal or Ward office desk).
   - Publication authorized in canonical catalogue with cryptographic SHA-256 evidence hash.
   - Includes PCMC (6 schemes), PMC (3 schemes), BMC (2 schemes), TMC (1 scheme), NMMC (1 scheme).

2. **`NO PUBLIC SCHEME INFORMATION FOUND IN THE SOURCES EXAMINED` (24 Corporations):**
   - Authoritative web portals examined: NMC Nagpur, NMC Nashik, KDMC, VVCMC, Sambhajinagar MC, Solapur MC, MBMC, BNCMC, AMC Amravati, NWCMC Nanded, KMC Kolhapur, AMC Akola, Panvel MC, UMC Ulhasnagar, SMKMC Sangli, Malegaon MC, Jalgaon MC, Latur MC, Dhule MC, AMC Ahilyanagar, CCMC Chandrapur, Parbhani MC, Ichalkaranji MC, Jalna MC.
   - Public portals actively offer civic services (property tax, water bills, birth/death records, BPAMS building permits) and facilitate Central/State schemes (PM SVANidhi, PMAY-U, Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar).
   - No autonomous, municipal-budgeted welfare schemes were found published on the public portals examined.
   - Strictly classified as `NO PUBLIC SCHEME INFORMATION FOUND IN THE SOURCES EXAMINED` rather than inferring that no schemes exist.

3. **`PARTIALLY VERIFIED` (0 Schemes):**
   - Reserved for programmes where gazette mentions exist but eligibility predicates lack specific income or residency cutoffs. No municipal scheme is currently published under this provisional status.

4. **`NOT IMPLEMENTED` (0 Entities):**
   - All 29 Municipal Corporations have verified directory records, source registries, and ward structures implemented.

All 16 local-tier and ULB-implemented schemes in the canonical catalog are backed by verified source records, official government portals, and explicit municipal administrative channels. They adhere to the same zero-compromise evidentiary standards as central and state schemes.
