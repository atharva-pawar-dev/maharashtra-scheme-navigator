## 1. Ten-Stage Scheme Lifecycle

Every government scheme discovered by Scheme Navigator must traverse an auditable, fail-closed ten-stage lifecycle before reaching citizen-facing recommendation:

```
[1. DISCOVERED]
       │
       ▼
[2. SOURCE EXTRACTED]
       │
       ▼
[3. SOURCE REVIEW] ──── (Low Confidence / Skewed OCR) ───> [RE-EXTRACT / AUDIT]
       │
       ▼
[4. POLICY VERIFICATION] ─ (Conflicting Sources) ────────> [LOG UNRESOLVED INQUIRY]
       │
       ▼
[5. RULE MAPPING]
       │
       ▼
[6. RULE TESTING] ────── (Boundary Assertion Failure) ───> [RULE REVISION]
       │
       ▼
[7. HUMAN APPROVAL] (Reviewer + Policy Analyst Signoff)
       │
       ▼
[8. PUBLISHED INFORMATION] (Signed by Catalogue Admin Key)
       │
       ▼
[9. CHANGE MONITORING] ── (New GR / Amendment Detected) ──> [SUSPEND OR RE-EVALUATE]
       │
       ▼
[10. SUSPENSION / SUPERSESSION / ARCHIVE]
```

## 2. Strict Role Separation & Security Controls

To prevent single-operator error, extraction hallucination, or unauthorized policy modification, Scheme Navigator enforces strict role separation under Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):

| Role Name | Granted Capabilities | Prohibited Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **INGESTION_BOT** (Automated System) | Fetch public government HTML/PDFs, compute SHA-256, run OCR/Gemini extraction. | CANNOT approve rules; CANNOT publish schemes; CANNOT modify database schemas. |
| **POLICY_ANALYST** | Review legal clauses, audit Government Resolutions, author rule boundary tests. | CANNOT authorize release signatures; CANNOT bypass security rate limits. |
| **DOCUMENT_REVIEWER** | Audit document extractions with confidence $< 0.75$, verify applicant certificates. | CANNOT access other citizens' threads; CANNOT sign scheme releases. |
| **CATALOGUE_ADMIN** | Review approval records, execute database migrations/backups, cryptographically sign release manifests. | CANNOT modify audit logs; CANNOT alter citizen session ownership tokens. |

## 3. The Evidence Hierarchy

When extracting and encoding eligibility predicates, the system enforces a strict hierarchy of authority:

1. **Gazette Notification / Operative Government Resolution (शासन निर्णय):** The binding legal basis. Supersedes all lower-level text.
2. **Current Departmental Guidelines & Statutory Rules:** Department-issued implementation directives.
3. **Scheme-Specific Operational Handbooks:** Procedural documents issued to field officers (Tahsildars, BDOs, Anganwadi Sevikas).
4. **Official Department Webpage:** Descriptive portal text.
5. **Application Portal Intake Instructions:** MahaDBT or Aaple Sarkar field validation forms.
6. **Departmental FAQs & User Manuals:** Explanatory material.
7. **Government Directories & News Releases:** Discovery leads only; never standalone proof of a statutory rule condition.

### Conflict Resolution Protocol
If two official sources conflict (e.g. MahaDBT portal form indicates a ₹2.5 Lakh income ceiling, whereas the base Government Resolution specifies ₹1.0 Lakh):
- The conflict is logged immediately as an **Unresolved Policy Inquiry (`POL-xxx`)**.
- The platform **does not invent an unwritten statutory exception**.
- The candidate scheme retains `rule_status: "draft"` and `publication_authorized: false`.
- The discrepancy is documented publicly in [docs/unresolved_policy_questions.md](file:///c:/Users/Admin/Downloads/scheme-navigator/scheme-navigator/docs/unresolved_policy_questions.md).

## 4. Fail-Closed Cryptographic Publication Gate

- **Default Invariant:** `Scheme.publication_authorized` defaults strictly to `False`.
- **Catalogue Quarantine:** The public rule engine automatically filters out any scheme where `publication_authorized == False`.
- **HMAC-SHA256 Release Signing:** Release to the active public catalog requires a signature generated by `CATALOGUE_RELEASE_SIGNING_KEY` over the canonical scheme JSON manifest (`src/scheme_navigator/security/publication.py`).
- **Draft Isolation:** All candidate schemes under `data/candidates/` remain isolated as drafts until explicitly signed.
